Title says it all.  
Cashless payments suck.

Back then, cashless payments were for irresponsible and careless customers as a workaround to be able to purchase stuff
without losing their money to robbers and other reasons.  Fair.
Now? Technology's evolved so much that people can just rob from your card / bank account directly.  

So now we are at a standstill. Both cashless and cash payments have the exact same drawback: risk of loss by malicious individuals.
And for whatever reason some stores are starting to go into cashless only and acting like cash payment is too tedious.

If you lose physical cash you just lose whatever you have on your body.  
If you lose credit card / bank account money? It's OVER.  

To this day we've got people who could've gotten jobs and money with their talent but they choose to do crimes with it instead.  
But the fact that we still have hacking incidents mean that the same level of talent is not appearing on the job market. Why?  
Blame HR LOL
